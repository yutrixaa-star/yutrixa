Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Yutrixa, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through our website, including information submitted through contact forms, consultation booking forms, careers forms, and other enquiry forms.

This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or business information
  • Project requirements
  • Messages and enquiries
  • Information submitted through careers applications

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Schedule and manage consultation appointments
  • Understand your project requirements
  • Provide and coordinate requested services
  • Process and evaluate career applications
  • Improve our website and services
  • Maintain business and project records

3. Information Sharing

Yutrixa does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Where necessary to provide a requested service, information may be shared with trusted service providers, technology providers, or project delivery partners who assist us in operating our business and completing services.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant business or project purpose.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website or services may use third-party tools and platforms for purposes such as appointment booking, communication, analytics, hosting, forms, AI services, payments, or other business functions.

These third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Yutrixa may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage, improve performance, and provide a better user experience.

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information provided to us against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect our legitimate business interests.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you.

You may contact us if you would like to request removal or correction of your personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses, professionals, and general users and are not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes that are prohibited by applicable law.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. Yutrixa is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, business practices, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, please contact us.
