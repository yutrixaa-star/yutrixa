Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear guidelines for advance payments, project cancellations, completed work and applicable refunds.

1. Advance Payments

Yutrixa may require an advance payment before beginning a project. The advance helps reserve project capacity and cover initial project activities and resources.

2. When Work Begins

Work is considered to have started when Yutrixa or an assigned delivery partner begins planning, research, design, development, setup, integration or other agreed project work.

3. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a client cancels before project work begins, Yutrixa may consider a refund of the advance after deducting non-refundable costs already incurred, subject to the project agreement.

4. Cancellation After Work Has Started

If a client cancels after work has started, the client remains responsible for the value of work completed up to the cancellation date, together with approved expenses and non-refundable third-party costs.

Example

Project value: ₹1,00,000

Advance paid: ₹40,000

Value of work completed: ₹55,000

Additional amount payable: ₹15,000

5. Refund Calculation

Where a refund is applicable, Yutrixa may consider completed work, work in progress, approved expenses, third-party charges and other non-refundable costs.

6. Milestone Payments

Payments for milestones already completed or substantially completed may remain payable even if the client cancels the remaining project.

7. Work in Progress

Work started but not completed may be valued according to the work performed and resources committed at cancellation.

8. Third-Party and Non-Refundable Costs

Hosting, domains, APIs, software subscriptions, plugins and other third-party costs may be non-refundable where the provider does not issue a refund.

9. Cancellation by Yutrixa

Yutrixa may cancel or suspend a project because of non-payment, repeated client delays, unlawful requests, abusive conduct or circumstances beyond reasonable control. Any applicable refund will be determined according to the project agreement and work completed.

10. Client Inactivity

If a client becomes unresponsive or fails to provide required information for an extended period, Yutrixa may place the project on hold or treat it as abandoned after reasonable notice. Amounts already earned and expenses incurred may remain payable.

11. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, Yutrixa will normally process it using the original payment method where reasonably possible. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

12. Exceptions

Project-specific proposals, contracts or written agreements may contain different cancellation or refund terms. In such cases, the agreed project-specific terms will generally apply.

13. Contact

If you have questions about cancellation or refunds, please contact Yutrixa before requesting cancellation.
